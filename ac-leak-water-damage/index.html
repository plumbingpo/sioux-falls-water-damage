AC Leak Water Damage in Sioux Falls: What to Know

Cooling a house also dries the air, which means the unit is constantly making water it must get rid of. In a healthy system the coil sheds it into a pan, and a narrow drain line carries it away. Once dust and algae block the drain, water backs up until it runs over the edge of the pan. With an air handler in the attic or a hallway closet, the overflow goes straight into the ceiling, wall or floor around it.

People tend to underestimate what a small, steady leak can do. The water wicks into drywall, soaks the platform under the unit and creeps under flooring, often with mold already started by the time a stain shows. Our team measures how far it traveled, dries the building materials and fixes the damage. An HVAC technician still has to fix the unit or unclog the line, and we plan our work around that appointment.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Turn Off the ACSwitch the system off at the thermostat so it stops making condensate. Flip the air handler's breaker too when water is close to its wiring.
  2. Keep Clear of Wet ElectricalCeiling lights, outlets and the unit itself are off limits while wet, and stay that way until their circuit has been turned off.
  3. Clear Water From the PanA pan you can get to safely should be emptied with towels or a wet vac, which ends the overflow. Skip this step if it means crossing a soaked attic.
  4. Dry the Easy SpotsMove clothes, boxes and furniture back from the stained area or the closet, catch ceiling drips in a bucket, wipe up hard floors and blot the carpet.
  5. Bring In Two ProsCall us to check how far the water spread, and call an HVAC technician to clear the drain line. Keep the AC off until the repair is made.
Why It Happens

How It Usually Starts

  • Units that rely on a condensate pump, when that pump quits or gets unplugged.
  • A drain line joint that came apart or was never glued, leaking inside a wall or attic.
  • The safety float switch being absent or broken, so the unit kept running.
  • More meltwater than the pan can handle after an iced-over coil thaws.
  • A rusted, cracked or out of level drain pan that lets water escape.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Closet Walls and Platform

In a closet, the unit usually rests on a wooden stand built above the return air box. The wood rots and the nearby drywall draws moisture upward, all of it out of sight in back of the unit.

Attic Insulation

Insulation around an attic unit soaks up overflow and holds it against the ceiling. Soaked sections usually come out, since they no longer insulate.

Ceilings Under the Unit

An attic air handler leaks onto the drywall beneath it. Staining is the early sign, sagging follows, and drywall left wet for days can come down into the room below.

Floors Outside the Closet

Carpet pad, laminate and hardwood near the closet all get wet from underneath as the leak spreads past the door. Laminate swells and will not recover, and hardwood cups if it stays wet.

Will Insurance Pay?

Insurance and AC Leak Water Damage

Timing is usually what decides whether an AC leak is covered. Water damage is paid for under many homeowners policies when the overflow was sudden and you responded as soon as you discovered it. Denial is possible when the adjuster concludes the leak ran for weeks or months, since that reads as a gradual or maintenance problem. The air conditioner repair is typically excluded in both cases. Check your policy, and we can supply readings and photos for the claim.

What We Do

How We Handle AC Leak Water Damage

1

Find Every Wet Area

We use moisture meters and thermal imaging around the air handler, the ceiling or walls next to it, and the flooring outside the closet to find every wet material, not only the stained ones.

2

Extract and Open Up

Our extractors pull water out of the pan, the wood platform and any carpet. Saturated or moldy insulation and drywall are removed, which opens the cavity to dry air.

3

Dry the Structure

Dehumidifiers and air movers stay on for a few days, usually 3 to 5, and we log the moisture readings. We place equipment so the HVAC technician can still reach the unit.

4

Clean and Restore

Affected surfaces get an antimicrobial treatment, because condensate that has sat for days is rarely clean. New flooring, baseboard, drywall and insulation follow, and fresh paint makes the area match.
