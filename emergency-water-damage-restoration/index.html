How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Sioux Falls

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
