Dealing With Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in Sioux Falls

A sump pump has one job, and it tends to fail at the moment it is needed most. During heavy rain the water table climbs and fills the pit, at which point an outage, a stuck float or an overwhelmed pump stops the pumping. The pit overflows and the basement floor starts to flood. Nothing stops it until some pump is working again.

Clean is the wrong word for what comes out of a sump pit. It has come through soil and often carries silt, bacteria and whatever was in the drain tile, so we treat it as Category 2 at best and Category 3 if it has sat or mixed with sewage. Cleanup is different as a result, with wet drywall, carpet pad and other porous materials generally removed and the rest cleaned and disinfected ahead of drying.

Your First Steps

  1. Keep Out of the BasementWith the electricity still on, a flooded basement is not safe to walk into. Outlets, the pump cord or the furnace might already be wet and live.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerIf the breaker panel can be reached while you stand somewhere dry, switch off the circuits for the basement. Call the utility when the panel itself sits in the flooded area.
  3. Check the Pump SafelySee if the plug pulled out, the outlet has tripped or the float got stuck on the side of the pit. Do not reach into the water.
  4. Treat the Water as ContaminatedWear rubber boots and gloves if you must go down, keep kids and pets upstairs, and wash your hands after touching anything wet.
  5. Save What Is Dry, Then CallGet dry valuables to a higher floor, snap photos showing the pump and how high the water is, and give us a call. Holding off until the basement empties is not the answer.
Our Process

Our Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Process

1

Get the Water Out

Because the pumps and extraction units are ours, the broken sump does not hold up the work. We clear silt and standing water off the floor, out of low areas and from around the pit.

2

Remove Contaminated Materials

Carpet, padding, insulation and wet drywall that the water contacted are pulled by a crew in protective gear, bagged and removed from the property so the concrete and studs are open.

3

Wash and Disinfect

An antimicrobial product goes on after the concrete, the studs and any kept belongings have been washed. Doing it ahead of drying keeps the air movers from spreading contaminants through the basement.

4

Dry and Verify

Dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture meter readings on the framing and concrete return to normal, typically 3 to 5 days. Before we pull our equipment, the pit should have a pump that works.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Few water claims confuse homeowners as much as this one. Water backing up or overflowing from a sump is usually excluded under a standard homeowners policy, and a flood policy is a different product with rules of its own. Coverage often exists only if you added a water backup or sump overflow endorsement, and those carry their own limits. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.

The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • Loss of power at the pump alone, from a loose cord or a tripped circuit.
  • Dirt and small rocks washed into the basin that lock up the pump.
  • Losing electricity in a storm when the pump has no battery backup.
  • Motor failure in a pump that has simply reached the end of its life.
  • A float switch that is stuck, tangled or pinned against the basin wall.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Stored Belongings

Books, cloth, boxes and wood furniture legs take in dirty water within a short time. Anything porous that sat in it is hard to disinfect and will grow mold within days.

Bottom Plates and Studs

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Drywall and Wall Insulation

Dirty water climbs from the slab into insulation and drywall, far higher than the line you can see. The lower part that got wet is cut away, not dried, on account of the contamination.

Basement Floor Coverings

Carpet and pad soaked by groundwater cannot be cleaned to a safe standard and are removed. Laminate and vinyl plank trap silty water underneath and usually come up too.
