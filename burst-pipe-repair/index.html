Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Sioux Falls Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Sioux Falls and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
